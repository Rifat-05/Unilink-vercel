About NSU Robotics Club

NSU Robotics Club is a student community focused on robotics, embedded electronics, autonomous systems, and engineering innovation. We compete in national rover challenges and conduct peer-led workshops for university undergrads to bridge the gap between classroom theory and practical lab engineering.

⚙️
